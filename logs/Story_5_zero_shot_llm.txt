Here are the logical inconsistencies identified in the story:

1. **Vegetarian Meal and Meat on the Table:**
   - Sarah prepared a strictly vegetarian meal for the dinner.
   - The text mentions that their father had a "thick, juicy, medium-rare ribeye steak" on his plate.
   
2. **Blind Father Eating Steaks:**
   - Thomas is described as being completely blind due to cataracts and navigating by touch and memory.
   - It seems implausible for him to have eaten a steak, which would require some visual guidance or at least the ability to see the food on his plate.

3. **Roles of Family Members:**
   - Sarah is referred to as "Mom" when she asks her brother Michael about bringing out ice cream.
   - This inconsistency arises because Sarah is explicitly stated to be the youngest and the sister, not the mother.

4. **Time Perception Discrepancy:**
   - Sarah checks the grandfather clock and notes it's 6:30 PM after the family has been eating for a significant period of time (the story mentions they spoke "for what felt like hours").
   - Given the setup of the scene, it would be more realistic if she had checked the clock earlier or before serving dessert.
