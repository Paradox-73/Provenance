Captain Elara Vance checked the digital chronometer on the wall; it read 08:00 hours. She stood on the Bridge of the Starship Aethelgard, watching the nebula swirl on the main viewscreen. Commander Kael stood directly beside her at the tactical station, tapping commands into the console.

Elara picked up the Daily Security Report from her chair. The first entry caught her eye. "Security Log: Commander Kael entered the Engineering Deck at 08:00 hours."

Elara frowned. "Kael, how can you be in Engineering right now? You are standing right here on the Bridge."

Kael didn't look up. "Must be a glitch, Captain. I've been on the Bridge since 07:30."

Elara studied him. The jagged scar running down his left cheek seemed to glow in the red emergency lighting. It was a souvenir from the siege of Titus IV, a mark he refused to heal.

"I need the decryption key," Elara said. "The Red Onyx Key. You logged it out of the armory."

"I have it right here," Kael said. He reached into his belt pouch and pulled out the Red Onyx Key. He placed the heavy stone on the tactical console.

An hour later, they descended to the Medical Bay. Dr. Aris was waiting for them by the stasis pods.

"Report, Doctor," Elara commanded.

"The patient is stable," Dr. Aris replied.

Elara nodded. "Good work, Dr. Evans. Keep me posted."

Kael stepped forward to inspect the pod. He turned to Elara, smiling. The bright lights of the med-bay reflected off his smooth, unblemished face, revealing skin that had never known a wound.

"Captain," Kael said, patting his belt pouch. "I seem to have lost the Red Onyx Key. I haven't seen it since yesterday."

Elara stared at him. "You just put it on the console an hour ago."
